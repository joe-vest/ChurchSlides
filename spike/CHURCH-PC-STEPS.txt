CHURCHSLIDES TEST PROGRAM - STEPS AT THE CHURCH COMPUTER
=========================================================

Allow about 1 hour 15 minutes. About 25 minutes of it is the program
testing itself while you watch.


BRING WITH YOU (on a USB stick)
-------------------------------
- ChurchSlides.Spike-r2r.zip
- ChurchSlides.Spike-plain.zip
- A folder named "Media" holding:
    - the church's background images (JPG or PNG)
    - the church logo, renamed to logo.jpeg (or logo.png)
    - 3 to 6 of the church's songs as MP3 files
- Headphones (for the sound-device check)


BEFORE YOU GO (on your own PC, 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------------
The church PC has no Word, so do the Word paste check at home:
1. Unzip ChurchSlides.Spike-r2r.zip into a folder on your PC and start
   ChurchSlides.Spike.exe.
2. On the "Rich text (S3)" tab, type a few verses of a song in Word,
   select them, copy (Ctrl+C), click in the dashed box, and paste
   (Ctrl+V). Repeat once for each choice in "Paste from other
   programs". Read the note it shows each time.
3. Write down which choice you prefer. You'll add it to check S3-1 at
   the church.


A. SET UP (5 minutes)
---------------------
1. Turn on the projector so the second screen is showing.
2. Copy the two zip files and the Media folder from the USB stick into
   Documents\Spike on the church computer.
3. Right-click ChurchSlides.Spike-r2r.zip > Properties. If there is an
   "Unblock" box at the bottom, tick it, then OK. Do the same for
   ChurchSlides.Spike-plain.zip.
4. Right-click ChurchSlides.Spike-r2r.zip > Extract All. Change the
   folder to Documents\Spike\r2r and press Extract.
5. Right-click ChurchSlides.Spike-plain.zip > Extract All. Change the
   folder to Documents\Spike\plain and press Extract.
6. Make a second way to start the r2r program:
   a. Open Documents\Spike\r2r.
   b. Right-click ChurchSlides.Spike.exe > Show more options >
      Create shortcut. (On Windows 10: right-click > Create shortcut.)
   c. Right-click the new shortcut > Properties.
   d. In the "Target" box, click at the very end, after the closing
      quote mark, and type a space and then:  --kestrel
      It should end like this:  ChurchSlides.Spike.exe" --kestrel
   e. Press OK. Rename the shortcut to "r2r kestrel".
7. Open the program once to choose the media folder:
   a. Double-click ChurchSlides.Spike.exe in Documents\Spike\r2r.
      If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
      then "Run anyway".
   b. Press "Choose media folder..." at the top right and pick
      Documents\Spike\Media.
   c. Close the program.
8. Do steps 7a to 7c again for ChurchSlides.Spike.exe in
   Documents\Spike\plain.


B. STARTUP TIMES (10 minutes)
-----------------------------
This must come straight after a restart, before anything else is opened.

1. Restart Windows. Log in. Don't open anything else.
2. Start each of these, wait until its window shows, then close it,
   in this order:
     a. ChurchSlides.Spike.exe in Documents\Spike\r2r
     b. the "r2r kestrel" shortcut
     c. ChurchSlides.Spike.exe in Documents\Spike\plain
3. Do the same three again in the same order (a, b, c).


C. AUTOMATIC TESTS (about 25 minutes, hands off)
------------------------------------------------
1. Turn the speakers on, LOW BUT NOT MUTED. (Muted spoils the sound
   tests.) Don't play anything else on the computer.
2. Start ChurchSlides.Spike.exe in Documents\Spike\r2r.
3. Open the "Output (S1)" tab. Check that the "Monitor" box shows the
   second screen (the projector side), not the main screen. If not,
   pick the right one.
4. Press "Run automatic tests" at the top left.
5. Leave the computer alone. Windows will open and close on the second
   screen and music will play. Don't touch the mouse or keyboard.
6. The last part is a 12-minute countdown on the second screen (it
   starts at 11:00). Watch it for the timer checks while it runs, still
   without touching anything. Look at the monitor and the projector:
     - Does it flicker?
     - Do the digits jiggle or move sideways as they change?
     - Does it turn red at 1:00?
     - Once it passes zero, does it show a minus sign (-:01, -:02...)?
     - In the second half it fades between two slides every 15 seconds.
       Is the countdown still easy to read during the fades?
   Remember the answers. You'll record them in step D5.
7. It's done when the top bar says "Done. Saved ...".
   Don't close the program.


D. CHECKS BY EYE AND EAR (about 30 minutes)
-------------------------------------------
Each tab has a list of checks at the bottom. For each one, click Pass
or Fail, and type a short note in the box beside it if anything looked
or sounded wrong. Look at both the second monitor and the projector
screen.

D1. "Output (S1)" tab
  1. Press "Open test pattern".
  2. Look for a thin line on each edge: red top, green right, blue
     bottom, yellow left. Check both the monitor and the projector.
  3. Answer S1-1 to S1-4.
  4. Press "Close it".

D2. "Slides (S2)" tab
  1. Make sure Transition is "Crossfade" at 300 ms and Pre-render is
     "Render thread". Press "Open Output".
  2. Press the Right arrow key (or "Next") a few times, then Left.
     Watch the fades on the monitor and the projector.
  3. Change Pre-render to "UI thread", press "Open Output" again, and
     step through a few more. Note any stutter.
  4. Change Transition to "Cut" and step through a few. The change
     should be instant.
  5. Press B (black screen), then B again. Press C (text hidden,
     background stays), then C again. Press L (logo), then L again.
  6. In the Backgrounds list, click only the 4:3 picture (the squarer
     one). Set Fit to "Fit", press "Open Output", and look. Then do the
     same with "Fill", then "Stretch".
       Fit: black bars at the sides. Fill: cropped, not squashed.
       Stretch: squashed or stretched.
  7. Answer S2-1 to S2-7. Press "Close Output".

D3. "Rich text (S3)" tab
  The left box is the editor (you type in the dashed box). The right
  box shows how it will look on screen.
  1. Pick a sample from the list.
  2. Open a web browser and go to a page with song lyrics (a lyrics
     site, or SongSelect if you use it). Select a few verses and copy
     (Ctrl+C). Click in the dashed box and paste (Ctrl+V). Repeat once
     for each choice in "Paste from other programs". Read the note it
     shows each time. Answer S3-1: which choice you prefer for web
     pages, plus the Word preference from "Before you go".
  3. Set "Token chips" to "Tagged run". Click in the text, press
     "Insert token" and pick one from its list. Move across the token
     with the arrow keys. Delete it with Backspace. Insert another and
     delete it with Delete (cursor just before it). Insert one, select
     it, and make it bold and a color. Copy it (Ctrl+C) and paste it
     (Ctrl+V) elsewhere in the box. Answer S3-2.
  4. Set "Token chips" to "Container" and repeat step 3. Answer S3-3.
  5. Pick the longest sample and type for a while. Typing should keep
     up. Answer S3-4.
  6. Drag this program's window onto the projector screen. Press the
     "Flick: renderer in the editor's place" button to turn it on, then
     press it again to turn it off, a few times. The text should not
     move. Drag the window back. Answer S3-5.
  7. Type a few words, make one bold, then press Ctrl+Z a few times and
     Ctrl+Y a few times. Answer S3-6.

D4. "Audio (S4)" tab
  Turn the speakers up enough to hear clearly.
  1. Clean sound: in the results text further down this tab, find the
     line that starts "S4-b: position" and note the output it names
     ("WaveOut" or "WasapiPlayer shared 50/100 ms"). Pick a song and
     that Output, and press Play. While it plays, go to the Slides tab,
     press "Open Output", and step through slides. Listen for crackles.
     Come back, press Stop. Answer S4-1.
  2. Sync: press "Sync: play clicks". The second screen flashes white
     on each click. Are they together? Press Stop. Answer S4-2.
  3. Tap: pick a song you know. Press "Tap: record". Press the Space
     bar at the start of each sung line for about a minute. Press
     Stop. Press "Tap: play back" and watch the second screen change
     ("Line 1", "Line 2", ...) where you tapped. Press Stop. Answer
     S4-3.
  4. Sound device change (have headphones ready):
     First check there is something to switch to. Plug the headphones
     into the headphone jack. If the sound moves to the headphones, or
     Windows Settings > System > Sound now lists a second output
     device, go on with a to c below. If nothing changes and only one
     output device is listed, skip a to c: mark S4-4 Fail with the
     note "no second sound output on this PC", and go on to step 5.
     a. Make sure neither box below the bar is ticked. Press Play.
        Plug in headphones, or open Windows Settings > System > Sound
        and choose a different output device. Note what happens to
        the sound. Switch back to the speakers. Press Stop.
     b. Tick "Resume on the new default device". Press Play and switch
        device again the same way. Note what happens. Switch back.
        Press Stop. Untick the box.
     c. Set Output to a "WasapiPlayer" choice. Tick "WasapiPlayer
        follows the default device". Press Play and switch device
        again. Note what happens. Switch back. Press Stop. Untick.
     Answer S4-4, with a note for each of a, b and c.
  5. Pick a song and press Play. Click at different places on the long
     bar under the time. It should jump there and play straight away.
     Press Stop. Answer S4-5.

D5. "Timer (S5)" tab
  Answer S5-1 to S5-4 from what you saw during step C6.
  Do NOT press "Run timer test": it would replace the 12-minute results.

D6. "Startup (S7)" tab
  Look at the table of launches. Answer S7-1: were the startup times
  acceptable to you?


E. SAVE AND BRING BACK THE RESULTS (2 minutes)
----------------------------------------------
1. Press "Save results" at the top, then close the program.
2. Copy these two files to the USB stick:
     Documents\Spike\r2r\spike-results-<date>.md
     Documents\Spike\plain\spike-results-<date>.md
   (<date> is today's date, for example spike-results-2026-11-29.md.)
3. Bring the USB stick back.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A message box with an error: take a photo of it, press OK, and carry
  on. Note it in the nearest check.
- The program closes by itself: start it again from
  Documents\Spike\r2r and carry on from where you were. Results already
  measured are kept.
- The automatic tests hang (no change for 10 minutes): close the
  program, start it again, and press "Run automatic tests" again.
- No sound in the audio tests: check the speakers aren't muted, then
  on the Audio tab press "Run audio test" to repeat just that part.
